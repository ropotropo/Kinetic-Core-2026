# Kinetic Core

> {{ROLE_LABEL}}. Current publication: **[{{CANONICAL_REPOSITORY_NAME}}]({{CANONICAL_REPOSITORY_URL}})**. [Publication game]({{CANONICAL_GAME_URL}}).

**Design a mission. Fly its critical moments.**

Created by **Mykyta Borisov** for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**, responding to **Space Mission Design Game**.

Kinetic Core is a playable spaceflight and spacecraft-design prototype. Players assemble a vehicle, see how its engineering choices affect performance, and fly the critical moments of a mission. Its connected Moon route retains the same custom spacecraft, mass, fuel and flight state from Earth launch to lunar touchdown.

## What is distinctive

The workshop is connected to flight: dimensions and components change mass, fuel, thrust and maneuvering reserves. Guidance uses the selected spacecraft's finite propellant rather than replacing a flight with a progress timer. The interface separates the continuous Moon mission from local practice, a separate docking approach and the Solar System atlas.

This is an educational game model, not a certified mission-analysis system. Sampling, lunar ascent, return and complete interplanetary transfers are future work. [Read the exact limits]({{WIKI_URL}}/Verification-and-Limitations).
