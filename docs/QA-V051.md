# v0.5.1 — Flight polish

15 November 2026. Sole game author: Mykyta Borisov.

## Scope

This patch addresses reported workshop framing, ascent camera instability,
launch staging guidance, horizon tiling, parent-body placement, above-ground
inspection and phone navigation. It adds a passive position map, direct Time
control and a globe destination rail. It does not add a continuous mission to
every selectable world or new EVA, sampling, lunar ascent or return mechanics.

## Verification

- Automated numerical checks cover stage depletion at caller rates 1×/10×,
  finite-fuel manual release, altitude versus actual orbit, critical-phase time
  caps, high-warp coast boundary stopping, workshop fit at several aspect ratios,
  bounded presentation shake, surface camera clearance and off-axis map framing.
- Production web and self-contained HTML builds use the locked Vite/Three
  versions. A fresh temporary dependency directory was used because an existing
  local Vite package had an invalid/evicted package manifest. Repository source
  and assets, rather than an older deployed bundle, supply the build.
- Browser checks and remaining limitations are recorded below after validation.

## Design and physical-model boundaries

The launch camera eases the visible attitude across an ideal-controller stage
change. It does not add forces or change fuel, trajectory or attitude telemetry.
Hardware-only bounds prevent animated exhaust from changing camera distance.
The thin atmosphere, Sun placement, coastal terrain, crater bowls and sky bodies
are illustrative scene art, not surveyed terrain or a new ephemeris model.
Solar-wing hinges are a visual deployment sequence; electrical generation and
structural flight loads are not simulated. Existing foot-contact reference and
custom hardware dimensions remain authoritative.

A fuller lunar arrival stage is specified in
[LUNAR-EXPERIENCE-NEXT.md](LUNAR-EXPERIENCE-NEXT.md).

## Final launch-site and exhaust pass

The fictional launch site now includes a trench/apron, marked access roads,
cryogenic tanks, pipe racks/valves, cable trays, small service buildings, masts,
lamps, trucks and crates. Repeated objects are batched: 17 additional instanced
site draws plus at most two trail draws, estimated from source structure rather
than measured GPU time. Site objects fade with the local terrain handoff.

The atmospheric trail uses 96 pooled puffs (32 under reduced motion) and a
short near-nozzle ribbon. Emission stops on pause, cutoff and countdown; visual
history ages out in roughly six seconds and tapers out by 50 km. SI emission
history follows the same corotating atmospheric frame used by the local site.
This is an illustrative exhaust effect, not a condensation/wind simulation or
new spacecraft forces. Browser checks verify appearance and shader operation;
physical-phone frame timing remains unmeasured.

The final site-layout correction removes obsolete road strips beneath the new
tank farm. A specialist checked 32 road/foundation footprint pairs with no
intersections, aligned road/apron top surfaces and verified continuous tank-leg
contact. The corrected pad was recaptured at countdown. Chase framing leaves
additional atmospheric trail room through a smooth altitude-dependent change.


## v0.5.2 pause/camera follow-up

The full-screen practice pause overlay intercepted pointer input intended for
camera tools. Its background is now pointer-transparent, its Resume button
remains interactive, and inspection hides that overlay while the header keeps
the explicit Resume action. Physics stays paused until requested.

exposes the icon-only Return to flight camera; resetting returns to External
chase and the flight remains paused. 117 tests and the production/portable
