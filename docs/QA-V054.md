# v0.5.4 — A live Moon on mission home

Sole game author: Mykyta Borisov. 15 November 2026.

The home-screen Moon is a real lit Three.js sphere using the existing NASA SVS
LROC 2K display-color map and 1K LOLA bump map. Its near side initially faces the
camera. Rotation is decorative at 0.003 rad/s, not a current lunar ephemeris.
The sphere is one draw / 3,968 triangles. No shadows or postprocessing are added.
The low-power renderer caps pixel ratio at 1.25 and draws at most 24 times/second.

The renderer is lazy, reused across home opens and released with the hub. It
stops while home is closed or the document is hidden, preserves rotation and
resets its timing anchor on resume. Foreground timing stalls are bounded to 0.1 s.
Reduced motion renders a static pose on open, resize or texture readiness without
an ongoing animation loop. The original image remains until the local color map
and a successful frame are ready, and returns on failure/context loss. The canvas
is decorative, non-interactive and hidden from accessibility navigation.

141 automated tests and production/portable builds pass. Four new tests verify
stopping/resuming, no hidden-time integration, draw cap, reduced-motion static
invalidations and cancellation after disposal. No flight physics or controls
changed from 0.5.3; its numerical and browser evidence remains in QA-V053.md.

Real browser checks: 390×844 and 1280×720. Texture readiness was true, the lit globe
was visible, repeated home close/open retained a single canvas, and the phone
canvas stayed 350×315 pixels at the observed 1× device scale. Existing controls
remained reachable. No recorded browser errors. Screenshots and logs are in
outputs/v0.5.4/. Final public-host and Safari checks accompany publication.

This is browser layout/rendering evidence, not measured physical-phone GPU
acceptance. Failure fallback, reduced-motion scheduling and visibility gating
are covered by source review and lifecycle tests; forced browser WebGL loss was
not injected. Native installers remain the separately labelled 0.5.0 release.
