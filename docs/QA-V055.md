# v0.5.5 — Fullscreen guide, navigation and coherent sunlight

Sole game author: Mykyta Borisov. 15 November 2026.

155 automated tests pass. Production and self-contained portable HTML builds
pass with the pinned Three.js 0.187.0 / Vite 8.3.8 toolchain. The build reports
the existing large-chunk advisory; this is not a measured phone-performance
result. Logs and browser captures are in `outputs/v0.5.5/` (local, ignored).

## Navigation and controls

- The fullscreen question-mark guide uses one actual scrolling body, a fixed
  header, portrait hero, direct biography and Play. Moon/Docking/Landing/More
  lesson IDs, model links and keyboard tabs remain intact. Direct biography,
  return focus, tabs, End navigation, Escape and Play were browser-checked.
  Play opens Missions. Opening and closing the guide during an actual running
  Europa descent left it paused with an explicit Resume control.
- A second agent reviewed the integration without edits. It found the old
  scroll-owner reset after deep More content; this was fixed. More → model
  sources → deep scroll → Moon now reveals the new panel below sticky tabs.
- Guide composition checked at actual 390×844, 320×740 and 1280×720 viewports.
  A 320 px text/helmet overlap was found and corrected with a vertically spaced
  portrait composition. Its final dialog width is 320 px; Play remains 118×46.
  The portrait retains the face and KC suit patch. Native desktop Safari also
  displayed the final guide/portrait and opened/closed its nested biography.
- Ready primary/next-maneuver actions use a slow soft-shadow pulse, with no
  expanding ring or scale. Disabled and secondary actions are excluded.
  Home Prepare expedition and enabled atlas Landing practice had the intended
  computed animation. Reduced-motion static treatment was source-reviewed,
  not tested through an OS setting change.
- Wide Workshop, docking and Solar headings were measured at x=640 on a
  1280 px viewport; the integrated Mission plan heading also measured x=640.
  Other shared header selectors were source-reviewed. Phone positioning stays
  within its existing layout rules.
- Legacy Mission payload cards replaced the visible dropdown. At 390 px all
  three cards fit without page overflow. Science package changed liftoff mass
  from 540.4 t to 542.4 t; Crew only restored 540.4 t. Buttons preserve native
  selection/change semantics and expose their selected state. No catalog mass,
  force or performance formula was changed.
- The Solar body rail changes the native selected body and actual 3D focus.
  Sun/Earth remain survey-only; Moon/Europa enable their exact local exercise.
  Earth-system End selected Moon; Europa started paused local descent at 800 m.
  Portrait atlas checks include 320 and 390 px. A requested landscape override
  did not propagate to the existing test tab, so no new 844×390 result is claimed.
  Earlier short-landscape flight evidence remains in QA-V051/QA-V053.
- Four new assembly-camera tests cover actual deck geometry, low pan/orbit,
  zoom and smooth return. A large downward orbit in legacy Earth launch setup
  stopped above the floor in a real browser. The shared deck height is 1.055 m;
  the camera and target retain 0.55/0.20 m clearance respectively.

## Sun and world frames

Seven new numerical regressions cover camera-independent bearings, local-frame
transforms, Moon-relative radial framing, sphere/height-field/visible-hardware
occlusion and bounded edge glare. Sun, foreground key and sky shading share a
fixed scenario direction. Surface parent planets no longer follow the camera.
This is an illustrative bearing, not live dated ephemeris; see PHYSICS.md.

An ordinary 1× Earth flight checkpoint at about T+119 s / 49.906 km was saved
through the UI, restored paused and inspected in the browser. Independent yaw
and pitch moved the Sun naturally through view. A small pitch aligned the real
rocket core in front of it: disk/halo/tag disappeared, then returned when the
core moved clear. Turning away removed the Sun rather than dragging it with
the camera. Telemetry remained fixed during inspection. In paused Io practice,
independent camera motion exposed the Sun and Jupiter at fixed sky bearings.
Screenshots record these states. No hidden simulation-state injection or time
advance was used. No browser errors were recorded for these checks.

## Scope and limits

The rotating home Moon and earlier actual stage-release, confirmed landing,
workshop, camera, plume and docking-detail work remain included; their evidence
is recorded in QA-V053/QA-V054. A fresh full Moon expedition and new full docking
capture were not repeated for this patch. Real-phone multitouch, GPU, thermal
performance and native installer runtime acceptance remain unverified. Native
installers stay separately labelled v0.5.0. The standalone HTML is build-verified;
this pass does not add new direct-file/offline Safari acceptance.

The temporary host is updated only after the exact commit is created. Its
manifest, active bundle and final browser/Safari smoke checks are recorded in
the local publication receipt. Existing presentation edits are excluded from
the game commit; media production receives the frozen source afterward.
