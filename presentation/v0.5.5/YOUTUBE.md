# Description

One spacecraft. Every critical moment.

Discover Kinetic Core, a space mission design game created by Mykyta Borisov, a 14-year-old creator from Ukraine, for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Choose a mission. Build your spacecraft. Fly the critical moments.

See how mass, thrust, gravity and limited fuel shape every decision, from Earth launch and manual stage separation to lunar descent and touchdown. This gameplay teaser also features spacecraft construction, flight instruments, a separate docking exercise and the KC flag ceremony.

The Moon expedition connects launch, transfer, lunar orbit and landing. Other worlds offer separate local landing rehearsals; the Solar System atlas is an illustrative exploration view.

Built for curious students and future explorers. Design the spacecraft. Fly the critical moments.

Sole project author: Mykyta Borisov.
Event: NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.
Challenge: Space Mission Design Game.
