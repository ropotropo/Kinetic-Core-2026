# Kinetic Core vector companions

This editable SVG family follows the primary mission-badge concept in `kinetic-core-mission-emblem.png`: black disc, white perimeter, arced project name, geometric KC, orbital ellipse and navigation star, and a lunar horizon with restrained Ukrainian blue/yellow bands. It is a **code-native companion interpretation**, not an exact trace or a claim of pixel-for-pixel equivalence to the PNG.

## Files

| File | Canvas | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kinetic-core-mission-emblem-companion.svg` | 1000 × 1000 | Editable mission-patch companion with black disc |
| `kinetic-core-kc-mark.svg` | 620 × 344 | Minimal white geometric KC on transparent background |
| `kinetic-core-horizontal-wordmark.svg` | 1600 × 400 | White KC and live Kinetic Core wordmark on transparent background |
| `kinetic-core-flag-3x5.svg` | 1500 × 900 | Black flag with centered vector mission emblem |

The flag is **3:5 hoist to fly**: 900 high and 1500 wide. Its emblem is centered at (750, 450), using a 720 × 720 placement box. The vector flag reuses the same geometry internally and does not embed or link a raster badge.

## Construction and editability

- All artwork consists of SVG paths, ellipses, circles, clipping, internal symbols, and live text. No external raster image, external stylesheet, web font, or network dependency is required.
- The KC letters are geometric vector paths and remain independent of installed fonts.
- The arced **KINETIC CORE** title and horizontal **Kinetic Core** wordmark are **live text, not outlined glyphs**. The arc uses individually positioned and rotated letters for broader renderer support. Their stack is `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`, consistent with the presentation. Glyph metrics can differ across platforms.
- For print/embroidery production, open the SVG in a vector editor, verify the selected font and spacing, then create an outlined copy. Keep these editable originals. Do not claim that the live-text files are already fully outlined.
- The orbit’s front segment has a black separation stroke before its white stroke so it reads as passing across the KC rather than merging into the letterforms.
- The companion family uses only black `#000000`, white `#ffffff`, Ukrainian blue `#0057b7`, and yellow `#ffd700`, plus transparent space. The blue band remains above the yellow band.

## Usage

Use the full badge when a classic mission-patch identity helps. Use the minimal KC or horizontal wordmark when the composition needs a quieter mark. White transparent assets are intended for black or comparably dark surfaces. Keep the original aspect ratio and do not stretch the flag or badge.

Leave clear space around the full badge of at least 10% of its diameter. For the minimal KC and wordmark, leave at least one KC vertical-stem width around the visible artwork. Keep the mark subordinate to the main cover title and do not overlap the author’s face or important gameplay controls.

Small reproduction can lose the thin orbital line, arced text, and lunar craters. Prefer the minimal KC at small sizes rather than shrinking every badge detail. The thin orbit and live title require production review before embroidery or fabric manufacture. Color hex values specify digital artwork rather than a certified textile or spot-color match.

These files contain the project identity only. They add no author names, event captions, institutional seals, sponsor marks, or visible AI captions.
